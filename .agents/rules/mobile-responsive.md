# Universal Mobile & Multi-Device Responsiveness Rule

- **Strict No-Horizontal-Overflow**: The site and all its components (headers, cards, tables, banners, modals, drawers) must NEVER exceed 100vw or cause horizontal scrolling on mobile screens (minimum 320px width).
- **Responsive Header**: On mobile, keep navigation, search, cart, and profile buttons compact and correctly padded. Do not display oversized subtitles or full text strings in the top navbar that cause layout breaks.
- **Adaptive Layouts**: Always test and design for all device widths: mobile (320px - 480px), tablets (481px - 768px), laptops (769px - 1024px), and large desktops (> 1024px).
- **Touch-Friendly Controls**: Ensure buttons and touch targets are easy to tap without overlapping or being pushed off-screen.
